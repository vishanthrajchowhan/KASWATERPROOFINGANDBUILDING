Project Overview

This Pensacola commercial property required a high-impact exterior refresh that addressed both appearance and performance. The project included professional exterior painting, pressure washing, window and door waterproofing, and full exterior weatherproofing to reduce moisture intrusion and improve long-term building protection.

What We Delivered

Pressure Washing

Exterior surfaces were cleaned to remove buildup, staining, and contaminants before finishing work.

Windows & Doors Waterproofing

Targeted sealing was applied to vulnerable penetrations to reduce infiltration and weather exposure.

Exterior Painting

Premium coatings delivered a brighter, more polished appearance while strengthening facade durability.

Building Weatherproofing

Comprehensive exterior protection reduced the risk of moisture intrusion and long-term weather damage.

Client Impact

The property now benefits from improved curb appeal, enhanced weather resilience, and a more professional exterior presentation that supports long-term asset value.

  • Reduced vulnerability to moisture and weather exposure
  • Improved visual quality for tenants, visitors, and stakeholders
  • Stronger, more durable commercial facade performance
